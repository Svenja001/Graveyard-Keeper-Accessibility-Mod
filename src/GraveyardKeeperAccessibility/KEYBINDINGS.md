# Keyboard reference

Every key this mod adds, grouped by where it works. These are **in addition to** Graveyard
Keeper's own controls — walking, attacking, interacting and the quick slots are unchanged.

The mod's own keys cannot be reassigned. The *game's* keys can: pause menu (Escape) → Controls,
where every binding is a row you can arrow through and change with Enter.

Anywhere "Ctrl" appears it means the left or right Control key; "Shift" means either Shift key.

---

## In the world

**Reading your situation**

- **H** — health and energy
- **R** — money
- **P** — technology points
- **Q** — day and time
- **G** — ratings for the zone you are standing in
- **Y** — what is in the four quick-use slots
- **J** — open the quest list (see below)
- **K** — scan the whole map for corpses

**Finding and reaching things**

- **Page down** / **Page up** — next / previous object in the current category
- **Ctrl+Page down** / **Ctrl+Page up** — next / previous category
- **Home** — say which object is selected and how far away it is
- **Ctrl+Home** — walk to the selected object
- **Escape** — stop walking (while the mod is walking you somewhere)
- **L** — walk to the way out of the current dungeon floor

**Fighting**

- **B** — turn combat assist on or off (auto-aim)
- **X** — turn auto-attack on or off
- **C** — attack the nearest enemy
- **V** — say what enemies are nearby and where

**Other**

- **U** — turn auto-eat / auto-drink on or off (off by default)
- **Ctrl+G** — audit the build zone you are standing in
- **Enter** — advance a speech bubble or a cutscene line

## In any window or menu

- **Up** / **Down** — move through the rows
- **Left** / **Right** — change the focused setting (volume sliders, options switchers, an
  ingredient's quality). On a row with nothing to change, they jump to the other side of a
  two-panel window instead — your inventory and the chest, the buy and sell lists
- **Enter** — activate the focused row
- **Hold Enter** — repeat an action that supports it, such as decomposing a whole stack at the
  study table in one go
- **Shift+Enter** — move or trade a whole stack at once, when a stackable item is focused
- **Delete** — destroy the focused inventory item, or delete the focused save slot
- **1** – **4** — in your own inventory, assign the focused item to that quick-use slot
- **Escape** — close the window (the game's own key)

---

## Dialogue with answer choices

- **Up** / **Down** — step through the answers
- **Enter** — say the focused answer

## Quest list (**J**)

- **Up** / **Down** — step through the objectives
- **Enter** — read the current objective again
- **J** or **Escape** — close

## Confirmation boxes (yes / no)

The question is listed as a row of its own, ahead of the options, so it can be read again after
you have moved to Yes or No.

- **Down** — reach the options
- **Enter** on the question row — read the question again

---

## Placing a building

- **Arrow keys** — move the building one step
- **Space** — snap to the nearest spot that would actually work
- **R** — rotate; **Shift+R** rotates the other way
- **I** — say the current position
- **Enter** — place it
- **Escape** — cancel

## Demolishing

- **Down** / **Right** — next object that can be demolished
- **Up** / **Left** — previous one
- **I** — say where it is
- **Enter** — mark or unmark it for removal
- **Escape** — leave demolish mode

---

## Fishing

- **Ctrl+F** — turn auto-catch on or off
- **Tab** — change bait, while the game is asking you to choose one

## Controls page (pause menu → Controls)

- **Up** / **Down** — go through the key bindings
- **Enter** — change the focused binding, then press the key you want
- **Escape** — cancel the change (this also closes the page)

---

## Notes

- A few of the mod's letter keys sit on keys the game also uses in specific modes — **R** is the
  game's rotate key while a building is being placed, and **I** opens the inventory. The mod only
  claims them where the game does not: **R** reads your money only when no window is open and you
  are not placing anything, and **I** reports a position only while a building is being placed.
- Keys that take a modifier ignore the plain version and vice versa, so **G** and **Ctrl+G** never
  fire together.
